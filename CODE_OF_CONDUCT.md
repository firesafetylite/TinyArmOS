# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TinyGPT welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing technical ideas without personal attacks;
- giving and accepting actionable feedback;
- respecting differing backgrounds, experience, and viewpoints; and
- prioritizing the safety and well-being of the community.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, trolling, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect reporters' privacy where practical, and may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

Project maintainers are responsible for applying these standards fairly. Instances of abusive enforcement may be reported to another repository owner or through GitHub Support.
